Appendix — Methodology, Explainers & Code Reference
Understanding Your Numbers
RMR (cunningham_measured_lbm): The cunningham_measured_lbm equation uses measured lean body mass as its primary input, making it significantly more accurate than Harris-Benedict or Mifflin-St Jeor. Formula (Cunningham): RMR = 500 + (22 × LBM in kg). Current LBM: 162.3 lb (73.6 kg) → RMR ≈ 2120 kcal.

TDEE: Total Daily Energy Expenditure = RMR × activity multiplier + TEF + NEAT. Moderate activity (3×/week) ≈ 1.3× RMR.

PSMF: A Monday protocol of ~1,200 kcal composed primarily of lean protein to accelerate fat oxidation while preserving lean mass. The 1,200 kcal floor is a hard limit.
Phase Structure Rationale
RESET (Wk 1–4): Establish baseline compliance, purge glycogen and excess water.
ADAPT (Wk 5–8): True fat-loss phase. Deficit tightens. Cardio added if fat-loss rate under plan.
CYCLE (Wk 9–12): Hormonal reset. Leptin, T3, cortisol normalize. Scale may rise 2–4 lb from glycogen refill — expected.
PEAK (Wk 13–16): Deepest deficit. Protein at maximum. Cardio at maximum. 4 weeks only to minimize muscle loss risk.
Calorie Taper Logic
Training calories are MONOTONICALLY TAPERED — contest prep never lets prescribed calories rise. The solver re-derives each week's numbers from the required fat loss, the Alpert fat-oxidation ceiling (×1.4 PED factor), and the remaining weeks to deadline. PSMF floor = 1,200 kcal throughout.
