## Appendix — Methodology & Reference

**PSMF floor:** `psmf_calories = max(calorie_floor, protein_g×4 + 250)` — never below protein needs.
**Front-loaded decay:** Fat loss: geometric decay to goal_fat_mass at deadline. Lean: linear regain toward lean_ceiling_lb.
**Monotonic taper:** Training calories never rise — the solver clamps each week to ≤ previous week.
**Alpert ceiling:** Max fat oxidation ≈ 31 kcal/lb-fat/day × 7 days / 3500 = 0.062 lb-fat/lb-fat/wk, ×1.4 with PEDs.
**Forbes p-ratio:** Fraction of deficit from lean mass; biased toward fat at high BF%. PED phase modifier applied.
