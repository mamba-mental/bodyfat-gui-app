Appendix — Methodology, Explainers & Code Reference
Understanding Your Metabolic Numbers
RMR (Resting Metabolic Rate): The Cunningham equation is used here because it takes measured lean body mass from InBody as its primary input — making it significantly more accurate than Harris-Benedict or Mifflin-St Jeor, which use total weight and height. Formula: RMR = 500 + (22 × LBM in kg). At 168 lb LBM (76.2 kg): 500 + (22 × 76.2) = 2,176 kcal ≈ 2,180 kcal.

TDEE: Total Daily Energy Expenditure = RMR × activity multiplier + TEF + NEAT. Moderate activity (3× training/week) ≈ 1.3× RMR = 2,834 kcal.

PSMF (Protein-Sparing Modified Fast): A Monday protocol of ~1,200 kcal composed primarily of lean protein to accelerate fat oxidation while preserving lean mass. The 1,200 kcal floor is a hard limit — going lower risks muscle catabolism at the current lean mass level.
Calorie Taper Logic (16-Week Progression)
Training calories taper from ~1,750 (Wk 1) to ~1,470 (Wk 16) following a front-loaded geometric curve — the sharpest drop occurs in Wks 1–4 (RESET) when glycogen and water loss accelerates scale movement. The curve flattens in CYCLE (Wks 9–12) during the diet break, then steepens again for PEAK. Rest-day calories taper in lockstep. PSMF floor remains 1,200 throughout — this is the metabolic floor given current LBM.
Why Fat Loss ≠ Weight Loss
Week 3 demonstrates this clearly: scale is 1.1 lb behind plan but BF% is 0.6% ahead. This means lean mass was preserved (or marginally gained) while fat was lost at target rate. A scale-only protocol would trigger an unnecessary caloric cut here. The BF% data (estimated from InBody or calipers) is the primary performance indicator; scale weight is secondary.
Phase Structure Rationale
RESET (Wk 1–4): Establish baseline compliance, calibrate caloric response, purge glycogen and excess water. Aggressive deficit tolerable because glycogen depletion amplifies scale movement without true fat-loss velocity.
ADAPT (Wk 5–8): True fat-loss phase. Body adapted to new intake. Compliance tighter. Cardio added if fat-loss rate under plan.
CYCLE (Wk 9–10 diet break + 11–12 re-entry): Hormonal reset. Leptin, T3, cortisol normalize. Prevents adaptive thermogenesis stall. Scale may rise 2–4 lb from glycogen refill — expected, not failure.
PEAK (Wk 13–16): Deepest deficit. Protein at maximum. Cardio at maximum. Minimum training volume to preserve strength. Duration limited to 4 weeks to minimize muscle loss risk at extreme deficit.
